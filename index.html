    Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?
    Yes. I work with businesses anywhere, including the UK and Ireland. We talk on WhatsApp, email or a video call, at a time that suits you.
    
      
    How do I pay?
    Half when we start and half before your site goes live, by a payment method we agree. The price is fixed before any work begins.
    
      
    Can I update the site myself?
    On WordPress and Shopify, yes: you edit text, photos and products yourself. For other builds I can add a simple editor if you need one.

    You do, in your own name, so the site is always yours. I set everything up for you. A simple site often costs only the domain, around $10 to $15 a year.
