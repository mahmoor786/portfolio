# Mahmoor, freelance websites and apps

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step, hosted free on GitHub Pages. Fonts from Google Fonts.

## Users

Small and medium business owners anywhere in the world (often UK and Ireland) who need a website, online store or app. They usually arrive from a link Mahmoor sent by email, or from his Fiverr or Upwork profile, and most open it on a phone. Many are busy or unsure what they need; some overthink and never ask for a quote.

## Product Purpose

Win paying projects for Mahmoor. A visitor should be impressed within seconds without reading, then understand what he builds, roughly what it costs, and send him a complete project brief in a minute.

## Positioning

A personal brand, not an agency. Fixed starting prices in USD, GBP and EUR, below what Western freelancers charge. Deployment (domain, hosting, SSL, launch) is included in every package, plus 30 days of free fixes after launch. The client owns the code, domain and hosting. Payment is 50% to start, 50% before launch.

## Operating Context

Clients reach out on WhatsApp or by email. A project brief form builds a ready-to-send WhatsApp or email message, so no backend is needed. Fiverr and Upwork forbid steering clients off-platform, so a separate work-only page (work.html) shows projects with no prices or contact details.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services: landing pages, business websites, custom WordPress themes, online stores (Shopify or WooCommerce), web apps, mobile apps (iOS and Android).

Packages (starting prices):
- Landing page: $299 / £239 / €279, 5 days
- Business website (up to 5 pages): $699 / £549 / €649, 10 days
- Custom WordPress: $999 / £799 / €929, 14 days
- Online store: $1,199 / £949 / €1,099, 3 weeks
- Web app: $2,499 / £1,999 / €2,299, from 4 weeks
- Mobile app: $2,999 / £2,399 / €2,799, from 6 weeks

No maintenance plans. No experience or education sections. WhatsApp number and email are placeholders until Mahmoor supplies them.

## Brand Commitments

Personal name "Mahmoor" only. Visitors should be impressed by looking, before reading. Dark, premium, gallery-like presentation chosen from three references.

## Evidence on Hand

No real client work, testimonials, reviews, logos or statistics yet. The work section shows concept projects by industry (gym, dental clinic, real estate, clothing store, café, fitness app), built in HTML and always labelled "Concept". Never invent clients, quotes or numbers.

## Product Principles

- Show, don't tell: working interface beats claims.
- Make the next step effortless: every price leads to a pre-filled brief.
- Be honest about what is concept work.
- Fast and clear on a phone first.
